Privacy Policy

How SEnuke AI collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information across our website, software, AI workflows, integrations and subscription services.

Effective: September 8, 2026  •  Last Updated: August 19, 2026

SEnuke AI — The AI Growth Operating System
This Privacy Policy applies to services operated by IATC Enterprises.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how IATC Enterprises, operating SEnuke AI (“SEnuke AI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information when you visit SEnuke.com, create or use a SEnuke AI account, interact with our software, communicate with us or otherwise use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

Such as your name, email address, account identifiers, workspace membership, role and authentication information.

Business and project information

Information you provide about businesses, websites, products, services, markets, customers, competitors, locations, strategies, projects and business objectives.

Client information

Agency users may provide information relating to their clients and client businesses.

Integration information

Information necessary to connect and operate third-party integrations you authorize, including account identifiers, access tokens, API credentials, website information, analytics information, search-performance data, ecommerce data, publishing information and other data made available through the integration.

Usage information

Information about how you use SEnuke AI, including features used, workflows performed, AI Capacity consumption, activity history, actions, approvals, errors and performance information.

Device and technical information

Such as IP address, browser type, operating system, device information, referring pages, log data and security information.

Transaction information

Information relating to subscriptions, purchases, renewals, refunds and transactions. Payment-card information may be collected and processed directly by payment processors or retailers rather than SEnuke AI.

Communications and marketing information

Messages, support requests, survey responses, feedback, launch-list subscriptions, newsletter preferences and interactions with marketing communications.

3. Information Generated Through the Service

SEnuke AI may generate or derive information from data you provide, including analyses, strategies, recommendations, reports, classifications, scores, opportunities, content, business insights and Next Best Actions. Some of this information may constitute personal information where it relates to an identifiable individual.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information to create and administer accounts and workspaces; authenticate users; provide requested research, analysis, generation and business-growth workflows; operate Business Discovery and growth-intelligence functions; generate strategies, recommendations and Next Best Actions; connect authorized integrations; manage AI Capacity; process subscriptions and transactions; provide support; maintain security; prevent fraud; diagnose errors; improve reliability; understand product usage; develop and improve SEnuke AI; send service and account communications; send marketing communications where permitted; comply with legal requirements; and enforce our Terms.

5. Artificial Intelligence Processing

SEnuke AI uses artificial intelligence to provide many of its functions. Information submitted to an AI-powered workflow may be processed by SEnuke AI systems and authorized AI model or service providers as necessary to complete the requested workflow.

We may use multiple AI providers and routing systems depending on task type, performance, reliability, availability and cost. Users should not submit personal, confidential or sensitive information that they do not have authority to process through the Service.

6. Agency and Client Information

Agencies may use SEnuke AI to process information concerning their clients. In those circumstances, the agency is responsible for determining whether it has the necessary authority, legal basis and permissions to provide that information to SEnuke AI.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, SEnuke AI may act as a service provider or processor on behalf of the agency with respect to certain client information.

7. Third-Party Integrations

If you connect SEnuke AI to a third-party service, you authorize us to access and process information made available through that connection as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Integrations may include website platforms, ecommerce services, analytics platforms, search services, payment services, email platforms and other business systems. You may disconnect integrations where supported. Disconnecting an integration prevents future access through that connection but may not automatically delete information previously obtained and lawfully retained.

8. Payment Processing

Payments may be processed by third-party retailers or payment processors, including JVZoo during applicable sales and subscription transactions. These providers may collect payment and transaction information directly.

SEnuke AI may receive transaction information necessary to provision access, manage subscriptions, process renewals, respond to refunds or chargebacks and maintain financial records. Third-party payment providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SEnuke AI and SEnuke.com may use cookies, local storage, pixels and similar technologies to maintain sessions, authenticate users, remember preferences, secure accounts, measure website and product usage, diagnose performance, understand marketing effectiveness and provide functionality.

Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

10. Analytics

We may use analytics technologies to understand website and Service usage, performance and customer interactions. Analytics information may include device, browser, page-view, event, referral, interaction and approximate-location information.

11. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information to service providers that supply hosting, infrastructure, AI processing, databases, analytics, communications, security, customer support and other services necessary to operate SEnuke AI; integration providers you choose to connect; payment providers; professional advisers; government or regulatory authorities where required by law; and potential or actual acquirers, investors or successors in connection with a legitimate business transaction subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal requirements.

12. Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

SEnuke AI does not sell personal information for monetary consideration as part of its ordinary business model. If our practices change in a way that triggers legally required opt-out rights, we will provide the required notices and controls.

13. International Processing

SEnuke AI serves users internationally and may use service providers operating in Canada, the United States and other countries. As a result, information may be processed or stored outside your province, state or country and may be subject to the laws applicable in those jurisdictions.

Where required, we use appropriate contractual, organizational or other safeguards for international processing.

14. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing the Service, maintaining legitimate business and transaction records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements and satisfying legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and applicable requirements. Information may also remain temporarily in backups or disaster-recovery systems after deletion from active systems.

15. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse. No online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and maintaining appropriate security for systems they connect to SEnuke AI.

16. Privacy and Security Incidents

Where required by applicable law, we will investigate privacy and security incidents and provide notifications to affected individuals and regulatory authorities when legally required.

17. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to personal information we hold about you; request correction; request deletion of certain information; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; object to or restrict certain processing; request portability where applicable; opt out of certain marketing communications; and submit a complaint to an applicable privacy regulator.

These rights are subject to legal exceptions and verification requirements.

18. Canadian Privacy Rights

Individuals subject to applicable Canadian privacy law may request access to personal information held about them and challenge its accuracy or completeness, subject to applicable exceptions. Privacy requests and questions may be directed to our designated Privacy Officer.

19. California Privacy

If SEnuke AI becomes subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (“CCPA”), qualifying California residents may have applicable rights including access or knowledge, deletion, correction, opt-out of certain sale or sharing, limitation of certain sensitive-information uses and freedom from discrimination for exercising applicable rights.

SEnuke AI does not currently operate a business model based on selling customer personal information.

20. European Economic Area and United Kingdom

Where European or UK data-protection law applies, processing may be based on grounds including performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent or compliance with legal obligations, depending on the activity.

Eligible individuals may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent, and may lodge complaints with the applicable supervisory authority.

21. Marketing Communications

Where permitted, we may send product updates, launch information, educational material, offers and other marketing communications. You may unsubscribe from marketing email using the unsubscribe mechanism provided in the communication.

Transactional, security, billing and essential service communications may still be sent where necessary.

22. Children’s Privacy

SEnuke AI is a business software platform and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has improperly provided personal information to SEnuke AI, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

23. Automated Processing

SEnuke AI uses automated systems to analyze business information and generate recommendations, strategies and other outputs. These systems are designed to assist business-growth activities and are not intended to make legally binding decisions concerning employment, credit, housing, healthcare, insurance or other regulated eligibility decisions about individuals.

24. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to SEnuke AI, our information practices, providers or applicable requirements. The “Last Updated” date will indicate when the policy was most recently revised. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.
